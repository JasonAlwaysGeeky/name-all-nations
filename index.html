Every key has a second floor, printed on the second line of its button: tap the same key again for that area's dense pocket — Central America · micro-states · Middle East · Caribbean · West coast · Pacific islands — and once more to come back out. On a touch screen the same six keys sit in a pad at the bottom-left corner; the ⌨ chip beside it folds the pad away when you want the whole map.

Small countries have a round button with a pointer to the shape — click the button; it retires once you're zoomed in enough to click the country itself. A numbered button is a dense area (the Caribbean, the Pacific, the micro-states…) — click it to zoom to the layer where each country has its own button. Dotted outlines group an island nation's islands and are clickable too: a rounded square when the islands cluster, a tilted pill when they string out across the sea (the Bahamas, Micronesia).
