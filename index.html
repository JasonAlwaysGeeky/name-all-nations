ClickTap a nation and type its name

The nation you pick lights up gold, and you type its name in the bar at the bottom. It turns green when you're right and flashes red when you're not. Spelling is forgiving: Brasil, luxemburg and Cote d'Ivoire all count.

Use the minimap to get around

It sits in the corner while you play. Click a zone or press its key: 1 2 3 across the north, Q W E across the south. Press again to zoom into the crowded area inside it. 0 shows the whole world.

It sits above the bottom bar while you play. Tap a zone to jump there, and tap it again to zoom into the crowded area inside it.

Choose a challenge and a mode

Place gives you a name to find on the map. Name asks you to type it, with hints and reveals if you're stuck. Hardcore takes those away. The clock waits for your first move, and the menu at the top pauses, restarts or switches mode.

On the map

  • A round button stands in for a nation too small to click, with a pointer to its shape. It retires once the nation itself is the bigger target.
  • A numbered button is a crowded area folded into one. Click it to zoom to where each nation has its own button.
  • A dotted outline groups an island nation's islands and is clickable too: a rounded square when they cluster, a tilted pill when they string out across the sea.
